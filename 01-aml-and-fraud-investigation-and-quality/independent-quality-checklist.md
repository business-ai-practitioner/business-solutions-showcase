# Independent Quality Assurance Checklist

## How to use

The reviewer should inspect the case inputs, source evidence and investigation record independently. Mark each criterion as:
- **Met** — sufficient evidence and reasoning are recorded.
- **Partly met** — some work is present but a gap remains.
- **Not met** — required work is absent or inadequate.
- **Not applicable** — the criterion does not apply, with rationale.

Do not convert these labels into a numerical quality score unless a separately designed and validated methodology is established.

## Common quality criteria

| Area | Review expectation |
|---|---|
| Case context | Reason for investigation and relevant context are clear |
| Data completeness | Relevant records and limitations are identified |
| Chronology | Events and transactions are accurately sequenced |
| Evidence traceability | Material assertions link to sources |
| Facts and explanations | Facts, customer explanations, inferences and unknowns are distinguished |
| Contradictory evidence | Conflicting information is considered |
| Investigative enquiries | Relevant questions are addressed or recorded as outstanding |
| Reasoning | Narrative follows logically from the available evidence |
| Conclusion | Provisional position is proportionate to the evidence |
| Documentation | Work is clear, complete and reproducible |

## AML-specific criteria

- Customer profile and expected activity considered.
- Incoming and outgoing activity assessed.
- Counterparty identities and relationships examined where available.
- Source and purpose of funds investigated.
- Customer explanation independently assessed.
- Movement of funds and unresolved destinations considered.

## Fraud-specific criteria

- Reported event and alleged fraud type clearly described.
- Event and payment chronology assessed.
- Customer account of events recorded without treating it as independently verified.
- Payment authorisation and customer understanding considered.
- Beneficiary and receiving-account information assessed where available.
- Relevant communications and external evidence considered.
- Unresolved issues and contradictory evidence recorded.

## Finding record

For every quality finding record:
1. Criterion.
2. What was observed.
3. Evidence/source reviewed.
4. Why the gap matters.
5. Required corrective action.
6. Owner and due date (if assigned by the organisation).
7. Closure evidence and reviewer validation.

## Independence

The quality reviewer must not merely accept the AI-generated investigation summary. The reviewer should challenge the analysis against source material and independently determine whether the work is complete and adequately supported.
