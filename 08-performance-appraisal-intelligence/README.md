# Performance Appraisal Intelligence

**Evidence before eloquence.**

An AI-assisted performance appraisal concept that helps managers connect employee contributions to individual objectives, team deliverables and leadership priorities — then identify evidence gaps before drafting an appraisal.

> **Project status:** Interactive concept demonstrator. The current public demo illustrates the intended user experience; it is not connected to an AI model, HR system or production assessment engine. All names, objectives and examples are fictional.

## Why this matters

Performance appraisals often combine several different questions in one document:

- What was the employee expected to deliver?
- What did they actually deliver?
- What evidence supports the manager's assessment?
- How did their contribution support team and leadership priorities?
- Are the proposed ratings consistent with the evidence?

A writing assistant can make an unsupported appraisal sound convincing. This concept takes a different approach: **assess the evidence and context first; draft second.**

## Explore the demonstration

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in a modern browser. No installation, account or API key is required.

The demonstrator lets you:
1. Review an illustrative team context and leadership priorities.
2. enter an employee's objectives, proposed What/How ratings and manager evidence.
3. see an illustrative evidence-readiness review.
4. preview a structured appraisal draft.
5. understand where human clarification and approval remain necessary.

The browser demonstration uses illustrative front-end behaviour only. It does not perform genuine AI reasoning, validate factual claims or determine employee ratings.

## Core capabilities envisioned

| Capability | Intended purpose |
|---|---|
| Performance context | Optionally capture team structure, team objectives, projects and Team Head annual objectives |
| Individual alignment | Relate an employee's objectives and contribution to relevant team priorities |
| Evidence assessment | Separate evidenced facts, manager assertions, interpretations and unknowns |
| Rating challenge | Flag proposed ratings that appear insufficiently supported or internally inconsistent |
| Appraisal drafting | Produce a structured, concise draft using approved organisational criteria |
| Team calibration support | Help managers review consistency across comparable roles and contributions |
| Human accountability | Keep rating decisions and final approval with authorised managers |

## Design principles

- Evidence before conclusions.
- Individual accountability distinguished from shared team outcomes.
- Missing evidence is not automatically evidence of poor performance.
- No invented achievements, development needs or strategic alignment.
- No automatic ranking, forced distribution or employment decisions.
- Configurable criteria; no assumed universal rating scale.
- Human review and approval are mandatory.

## Scope and boundaries

This is an independent portfolio concept using synthetic information. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or based on confidential frameworks from any employer.

The demonstrator does not:
- connect to employee or HR records;
- make or recommend employment decisions;
- infer protected characteristics;
- establish that manager-reported metrics are independently verified;
- provide legal, HR or industrial-relations advice.
