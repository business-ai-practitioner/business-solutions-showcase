# Management Intelligence

## Objective

Convert consistently recorded quality findings into useful operational insight without overstating what the data shows.

## Suggested quality themes

- Incomplete customer profile assessment.
- Weak counterparty analysis.
- Insufficient source-of-funds assessment.
- Unclear purpose of payments.
- Incomplete fraud event chronology.
- Weak beneficiary analysis.
- Unsupported or premature conclusions.
- Missing evidence references.
- Unaddressed contradictory information.
- Incomplete documentation.

## Suggested reporting dimensions

- Investigation pathway: AML or fraud.
- Business unit or team (only where appropriate and privacy-safe).
- Quality theme.
- Finding severity, if defined by an approved methodology.
- Root-cause category, where evidenced.
- Corrective action status.
- Repeat finding indicator.
- Period of review.

## Interpretation safeguards

- A count of findings is not automatically a measure of investigator performance.
- Different case complexity and sampling approaches affect comparisons.
- Small samples should not be presented as reliable trends.
- Findings should be validated and consistently classified.
- Avoid exposing customer or employee identifiers in management reporting.
- Any trend, rate or performance claim must be calculated from a defined and suitable dataset.

## Prototype status

The current prototype can display findings for an individual synthetic case. It does not yet implement a multi-case trend dashboard or validated management metrics.
