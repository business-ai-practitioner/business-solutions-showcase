# Demonstration Guide

## Purpose

This walkthrough demonstrates the intended experience using fictional information and simple illustrative browser-side checks. It is not a live AI assessment.

## Suggested 3-minute walkthrough

### 1. Start with the business problem

Appraisal writing is not the hardest part. The harder task is establishing whether the assessment is fair, aligned to expectations and supported by evidence.

### 2. Show the context layer

Open the optional team context and explain the hierarchy:

**Team Head priorities → team objectives and projects → individual objectives → employee contribution.**

Context is optional. Shared team outcomes must not automatically be attributed to every team member.

### 3. Enter individual evidence

Use the fictional sample employee and manager notes. The proposed Results/What and Behaviours/How ratings are inputs for human review, not decisions made by the tool. Organisations would configure their own rating terminology and definitions.

### 4. Review the evidence panel

Show the difference between measurable details, manager-reported benefits that still need validation, broad behavioural language and missing context. The current checks use simple keyword and pattern matching; they are not validated assessments.

### 5. Preview the draft

Show the structured draft and clarification questions. Explain that any future AI implementation must not introduce claims absent from the source evidence.

### 6. Close with accountability

The manager remains responsible for checking facts, resolving flags, determining ratings and approving the appraisal.

## Suggested narration

"Most appraisal tools help managers write better sentences. This concept asks a more important question first: are the sentences justified? It can also connect individual objectives to team work and leadership priorities where that context is available. The aim is not to automate judgement, but to make evidence, gaps and human decisions more visible."

## Demonstration limitations

The current page does not call an AI model, persist records, authenticate users, integrate with HR systems or perform validated rating analysis. Do not use real employee information.
