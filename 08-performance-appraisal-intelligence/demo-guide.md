# Demonstration Guide

## Purpose

This walkthrough demonstrates the intended experience of Performance Appraisal Intelligence. It uses fictional information and illustrative interface behaviour.

## Suggested 3-minute walkthrough

### 1. Start with the business problem

Explain that appraisal writing is not the hardest part. The harder part is establishing whether the assessment is fair, aligned to expectations and supported by evidence.

### 2. Show the context layer

Open the optional team context. Explain the hierarchy:

**Team Head priorities → team objectives and projects → individual objectives → employee contribution.**

Emphasise that context is optional and that shared team outcomes must not automatically be attributed to every team member.

### 3. Enter individual evidence

Use the sample employee and manager notes. Point out that the manager's proposed ratings are inputs to be examined, not decisions made by the tool.

### 4. Review the evidence-readiness panel

Show the difference between:
- a specific example;
- a manager-reported metric that still needs verification;
- a broad behavioural statement;
- an unknown or missing fact.

The public demonstrator uses simplified illustrative checks. Do not describe these as validated AI assessments.

### 5. Preview the draft

Show how a draft can be structured around delivery, contribution, behaviours and development. Explain that the final draft must not introduce claims that were absent from the source evidence.

### 6. Close with accountability

The manager remains responsible for checking the facts, resolving flags, determining the rating and approving the appraisal.

## Suggested narration

"Most appraisal tools help managers write better sentences. This concept asks a more important question first: are the sentences justified? It can also connect individual objectives to the team's work and the Team Head's annual priorities, where that context is available. The aim is not to automate judgement. It is to make the evidence, gaps and human decisions more visible."

## Demonstration limitations

The current public page is a front-end concept demonstrator. It does not call an AI model, persist records, authenticate users, integrate with HR systems or perform validated rating analysis.
