# Illustrative investigation and quality outputs

These examples show the style of output a practitioner should expect. They are based only on the synthetic case information in this repository. They are not actual bank decisions.

## AML example — provisional assessment

**Recorded facts**
- The supplied account data shows multiple incoming payments from different individuals.
- The supplied data also shows outgoing transfers during the review period.

**Customer statement**
- The customer says the incoming funds are repayments of personal loans.

**Observations**
- The activity includes payments from multiple parties and onward transfers.
- The supplied case information does not establish the relationship with each payer or recipient.

**What remains unknown**
- Whether the payments are genuinely loan repayments.
- The source of the funds paid into the account.
- The purpose and context of the outgoing transfers.

**Proportionate next enquiries**
- Establish the relationship with each payer and beneficiary.
- Obtain and assess supporting information for the stated loan repayments.
- Clarify the purpose of the outgoing transfers and whether they relate to the incoming payments.

**Provisional position**
Further enquiries are required. The information supplied is not sufficient to establish the source and purpose of the material funds or reach a definitive conclusion.

## Fraud example — provisional assessment

**Recorded facts**
- The supplied case records three payments made by the customer to a new beneficiary.
- The customer reported the incident after being contacted by a caller claiming to represent the bank.

**Customer statement**
- The customer says the caller instructed them to move funds to a safe account.

**Observations**
- The reported contact preceded the payments.
- The customer says they authorised the payments because of the caller's instructions.

**What remains unknown**
- The caller's identity and contact origin.
- Whether the beneficiary account is connected to the reported scam.
- Whether funds were moved onward and what recovery steps were possible.

**Proportionate next enquiries**
- Preserve and review available contact and authentication records.
- Establish the beneficiary details and available onward movement information.
- Record the timing and outcome of the bank's fraud response and recovery enquiries.

**Provisional position**
The reported circumstances are consistent with a suspected impersonation scam, but the identity of the caller and destination of funds remain to be established. The bank's authorised fraud process must determine the case outcome.

## Quality challenge example

**Finding:** The investigator records that the customer received payments as loan repayments but does not establish the relationship with the payers or obtain supporting information.

**Why it matters:** The explanation has not been independently assessed. The investigation conclusion may therefore rely on an unverified statement.

**Required correction:** Identify each payer, establish the relationship, assess proportionate supporting evidence and update the case rationale to reflect what is verified and what remains unknown.

**Closure evidence:** Updated investigation record with source references, assessment of the explanation and a reasoned provisional position.

## Important distinction

The local prototype displays predefined observations and flags. It does not independently generate or validate the narrative above. These examples illustrate the intended quality of human investigation writing.
