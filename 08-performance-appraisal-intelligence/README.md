# Performance Appraisal Intelligence

**Evidence before eloquence.**

A business-led concept for improving performance appraisals by connecting individual contribution to agreed objectives, team deliverables and leadership priorities, then identifying evidence gaps before drafting.

> **Current status:** Interactive concept demonstrator. The page contains illustrative client-side checks only. It does not call an AI model, verify facts, determine ratings, store employee data or connect to HR systems. All examples are fictional.

## Why this matters

Performance appraisals combine several questions: what was expected, what was delivered, what evidence supports the assessment, how individual work contributed to shared outcomes, and whether the proposed assessment is consistent with the applicable criteria. A writing assistant can make unsupported claims sound convincing. This concept puts evidence and context before drafting.

## Try the demonstration

Open the [live demonstration](https://business-ai-practitioner.github.io/business-solutions-showcase/08-performance-appraisal-intelligence/) in any modern browser. No installation, account or API key is required.

The demonstrator lets you:
1. Enter fictional employee objectives and manager observations.
2. Optionally add team structure, team objectives, projects and Team Head priorities.
3. View simple illustrative pattern checks and clarification prompts.
4. Preview and copy a structured draft.

**Important:** These checks are basic browser-side pattern matching, not validated evidence analysis or AI reasoning. Do not enter real employee information.

## Intended capabilities

- **Performance context:** optional team structure, team objectives, projects and leadership priorities.
- **Individual alignment:** connect employee objectives and contributions to wider expectations.
- **Evidence assessment:** distinguish documented facts, manager assertions, interpretations and unknowns.
- **Rating challenge:** flag unsupported or inconsistent proposed assessments for human review.
- **Appraisal drafting:** prepare a structured draft using the organisation's configured criteria.
- **Team calibration support:** help authorised managers examine consistency across comparable roles without automated ranking or forced distribution.

## Design principles

- Evidence before conclusions.
- Individual accountability distinguished from shared team outcomes.
- Missing evidence is not automatically evidence of poor performance.
- No invented achievements, development needs or strategic alignment.
- No automatic ranking, forced distribution or employment decisions.
- Rating terminology and criteria must be configurable; Results/What and Behaviours/How are illustrative labels only.
- Human review and approval remain mandatory.

## Scope and boundaries

This is an independent portfolio concept using synthetic information. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or based on confidential frameworks from any employer. The demonstrator does not connect to employee records, infer protected characteristics, verify manager-reported metrics or provide legal, HR or industrial-relations advice.
